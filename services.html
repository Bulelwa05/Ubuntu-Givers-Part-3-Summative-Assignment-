Our Community Programmes

Ubuntu Givers runs several programmes designed to support individuals and strengthen communities.

Showing all 5 programmes.

Education Support Programme

This programme provides after-school tutoring, homework support, and educational resources to learners who need additional academic assistance.

Food Relief Initiative

The Food Relief Initiative provides food parcels and community meals to families and individuals experiencing food insecurity.

Digital Skills Academy

The Digital Skills Academy introduces community members to basic computer skills, digital literacy, and technology awareness.

Youth Leadership and Mentorship

This programme provides young people with mentorship, career guidance, leadership development, and personal development opportunities.

Community Development Projects

Community development projects include donation drives, seasonal outreach campaigns, and initiatives designed to improve local community spaces.
